# Pre-registration: mechanism experiment (written BEFORE any mechanism result was computed)
Estimand: Delta_h = C_pooled - C_HCCR (positive: HCCR cheaper), per simulation, mean over 4 regions and 272 test origins, ungated policies (gate excluded to isolate calibration), AR regime, paired by seed with common random numbers (same underlying draws across all cells).
Factors: phi in {0.95, 0.0} (stationary latent sd fixed at 10% of level; phi=0 gives an iid latent); window in {natural (ends d-h), anticipative (ends d-1; uses future demand; DIAGNOSTIC, non-deployable)}; h in {1, 14, 28} (h=1: sanity check, anticipative == natural by construction).
PRIMARY contrast (h=14 and h=28 reported separately; Holm over the 2): Theta_h = [Delta_h(ant)-Delta_h(nat)] at phi=0.95 minus [Delta_h(ant)-Delta_h(nat)] at phi=0.
Reading: Theta_h > 0 (CI excludes 0) supports "staleness hurts HCCR more when residuals are persistent". CI including 0 = inconclusive (not evidence of absence).
Secondary / exploratory (uncorrected, labelled): (i) dose-response of Delta_1 in extra lag L in {7,14,28} (deployable); (ii) window length W in {100,400} vs 200 at phi=0.95 natural (finite-sample alternative); (iii) fine reserve pool (64 sets, unit capacity steps, linear cost 0.7/unit) at phi=0.95 natural (quantization alternative); (iv) disagreement rate, selected capacity, reserve cost, unmet, lag-h residual autocorrelation, approximate effective window size W(1-r1)/(1+r1), 80% interval coverage.
Directional predictions (fixed now): H-delay+H-persistence => Theta_14, Theta_28 > 0. Quantization alternative => |Delta| larger and disagreement higher under the fine pool at all h. Finite-sample alternative => reversal at h=28 shrinks with W=400. If none move: inconclusive for mechanism.
Inference: paired per-simulation differences; 95% percentile bootstrap (10,000 resamples, seed 12345) over simulations; Holm over the 2 primary contrasts; minimum practically meaningful effect (a DESIGN CHOICE, not an observation): 0.10 cost units; power 0.80; alpha 0.05.
Sample size rule: pilot of 30 simulations (seeds 200-229) is used ONLY to estimate the paired sd of Theta_h and compute N with mechanism/power.py (simulated_power on the pilot's paired differences); the confirmatory run uses fresh seeds (300+) and N from that rule, capped by compute; if the cap binds, the result is reported as underpowered. No peeking at confirmatory results before N is reached.
Exclusions: only numerical/solver failures, counted and reported. Anticipative rows are never aggregated into deployable-policy statements.
